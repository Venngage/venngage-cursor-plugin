# Venngage for Cursor and Grok Bot

Turn your data, notes and documents into professional, fully editable Venngage designs without leaving your chat. Share your content, explain what you need, and Venngage creates an infographic, brochure, report, one-pager and more. Every design is saved to your Venngage account, where you can edit, download or share it.

## What you need

- A Venngage account. Don't have one? You can sign up for free while you connect.

## Connect

1. Install the Venngage plugin from the Cursor Marketplace.
2. When prompted, sign in to Venngage. New to Venngage? Sign up with your email or Google account.
3. Review the permissions and approve access.

In Grok Bot, you'll find Venngage under **Connect apps** once the plugin is installed. On a team plan, your admin may need to allow the plugin first.

## Example prompts

- "Use Venngage to turn this report into a one-page summary infographic."
- "Make an infographic showing these survey results with Venngage."
- "Use Venngage to create a timeline of our product launch: research in January, beta in March, launch in June."
- "Use Venngage to design a brochure for a dental clinic covering services, our team and how to book."

Each design takes about 1 to 3 minutes. When it's ready, you'll get a preview and a link to open it in the Venngage editor.

## Edit your design

Each request creates a new design in your Venngage account. Open the link to edit any element in the Venngage editor, keep refining with Venngage's AI, check accessibility, and download or share your design.

## AI credits

Each design uses Venngage AI credits. Every plan includes daily AI credits, with more on paid plans. See [Venngage plans](https://venngage.com/pricing).

## Troubleshooting

- **Can't connect or sign in:** allow pop-ups in your browser and try again.
- **Can't find your design:** make sure you're signed in to the same Venngage account you connected.
- **Out of AI credits:** credits refresh daily. See [Venngage plans](https://venngage.com/pricing).
